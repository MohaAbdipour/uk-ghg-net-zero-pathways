## Final UK greenhouse gas emissions statistics, 1990 to 2024

- Publisher: Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ)
- Publication: Final UK greenhouse gas emissions statistics: 1990 to 2024
- Published: 5 February 2026
- Updated: 25 June 2026
- Status: Accredited Official Statistics
- Landing page: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/final-uk-greenhouse-gas-emissions-statistics-1990-to-2024
- Workbook: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6982294819d3abdb495f37ce/final-greenhouse-gas-emissions-tables-2024.xlsx
- Local source file: `data/raw/final-greenhouse-gas-emissions-tables-2024.xlsx`
- SHA-256: `39849813643d6ed812c10030fb4fdcba98e598d7f8a890a446450c74baba66fa`
- Retrieved: 1 October 2026

Tables used:

- Table 1.2: UK territorial greenhouse gas emissions by source category, 1990-2024.
- Table 2.2: progress towards UK international greenhouse gas reduction targets under the Paris Agreement.
- Table 3.3: complete 1990-2024 emissions series for the Paris Agreement reporting scope.
- Table 4.2: uncertainty by source sector. This table covers the UK, Crown Dependencies and Overseas Territories and will not be treated as UK-only uncertainty.

The release states that the 1990-2024 series has been revised and supersedes earlier editions. Source-sector emissions allocate emissions to the sector where they occur.

## UK 2035 Nationally Determined Contribution

- Publisher: DESNZ
- Published: 30 January 2025
- URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uks-2035-nationally-determined-contribution-ndc-emissions-reduction-target-under-the-paris-agreement
- Target: at least 81% below 1990 levels by 2035, excluding international aviation and shipping.

The same page confirms that the 2030 target of at least 68% below 1990 levels remains in place.
